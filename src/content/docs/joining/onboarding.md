Once you receive the invitation to join the CAPIBARA Collaboration, you will need to get started on our tools to become a full member.

:::note[Membership Agreement]
Depending on your contribution and/or situation, you may need to sign a membership agreement. This process is handled before the invitation to join by a team manager.
:::

We use [GitHub](https://github.com) as our information hub. All our documents, code, and planning lives in our organisation: [CAPIBARA3](https://github.com/capibara3). GitHub has lots of great tools such as repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, that make remote collaboration easier. This might fele overwhelming at first, but don't panic (!), you will learn all this in a tutorial later on.

To access all of our files, you will need to join the organisation. First, you will need to [create a GitHub user](https://docs.github.com/en/get-started/start-your-journey/creating-an-account-on-github) if you haven't yet done so.

Reply to the email in which you received the invitation to join with the following information:
- GitHub username: to invite you to the GitHub organisation
- Short statement, CV, motivation letter (any documentation you may want to share for us to assign you to a team/task)
- Depending on the team that you are joining, you may need to provide more information. Pay attentian to the invitation email.

:::caution[Privacy Policy]
Providing this data is optional and you may request not to send, to modify, or to delete your data anytime. By sending the data, you agree to it being treated as outlined in our [privacy policy](../governance/25_PRIVACY_POLICY.md). It will be hosted on GitHub, and depending on the repository it is written in, be publicly available including through the CAPIBARA Collaboraiton's website.
:::

:::tip[Important Documentation]
Upon becoming a member of the collaboration, you may want to read the most important documentation for best practices in the _governance_ section.
:::

:::tip[Reach out]
Welcome to the team! Remember, you can always ask and reach out to older team members. We are all here to learn from each other!
:::
