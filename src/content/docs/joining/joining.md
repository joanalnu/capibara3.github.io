## Requirements

There are no technical requirements to apply to the CAPIBARA Collaboration and the application process is there, not to evaluate you, but to get to know each other and assign you to a group or specific tasks. Please, be reminded that the CAPIBARA Collaboration accepts applications from all fields and we are looking forward to learn how you want to contribute to our project. The following requirements are kept to a minimum, for example: english is fundamental for communication in international teams.

- **Strong English Communication (oral/written)** is required for collaboration and outreach
- You must **be a student** from any level: high school to PhD. CAPIBARA sends a strong message since it is fully run by students, originally founded by high schoolers.
- **Be interested in a related field(s)**: You don't have to bring expertise already, you are here to learn! However, having an interest or topic you want to pursue makes it easier for you to contribute to the project. It doesn't need to be STEM (physics, engineering), we are also looking for artists, social media creators, ...


Recommended, but **NOT** required: strong academic record, experience in a specific field, GitHub/Python knowledge. These are not required, if you know them you will get started faster, but if don't we'll teach you!


## Steps

You totally or partially fulfil the requirements? Then you should apply!


There is no selection process during the application, everyone who applies and fulfils the basic requirements can join, and we are happy to have you onboard.

To apply, visit our [application form](https://capibara-collaboration.pages.dev/join-us) and fill in the form. Then we'll get in touch with you via email to manage the onboarding.
