CAPIBARA-GAMMA is a student-led mission concept to observe gamma-ray bursts (GRBs) and advance our understanding of fundamental physics, in the era of multi-messenger astrophysics.

## Roadmap

The programme follows a deliberate growth path:

1. **Pathfinder (2024–2026, foundation phase):** a single CubeSat to prove the concept.
2. **Dual-satellite demonstrator (~2030):** two CubeSats whose timing enables localisation of transients by triangulation.
3. **Constellation (2032–2035):** an operational network of four microsatellites.

## What we work on

- **Science:** GRB analysis, cosmology research, detector characterisation.
- **Hardware:** detector design, electronics, mechanical engineering and testing.
- **Software:** simulations, data-analysis pipelines, mission control software.
- **Operations:** mission planning, satellite operations, ground station work.
