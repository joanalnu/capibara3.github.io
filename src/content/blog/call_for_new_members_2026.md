As we enter the new year, CAPIBARA is opening applications for students to collaborate across our active research and engineering initiatives.

This is a volunteer opportunity open to students at all education levels (High School, Undergraduate, Master’s, PhD). We welcome candidates from physics, engineering, computer science, art, legal, communication, and education.

> Active Programme Highlight \
> The **CAPIBARA-COSMOS** programme is currently actively recruiting for its engineering team. Check out the COSMOS Programme for details.



## Requirements

We aim to keep barriers to entry as low as possible and do not conduct selective screening. Every student with genuine motivation is welcome if they meet basic criteria:

- **Good Working English:** Required for international team meetings and documentation.
- **Student Status:** High school, undergraduate, or graduate.
- **Time & Dedication:** Ability to join occasional syncs and work on self-paced tasks.

(_Recommended, but NOT required: prior research experience, coding skills, or advanced math knowledge—you will learn on the job!_)

## How to Apply

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis with no strict deadline.
